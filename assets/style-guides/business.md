# Business translation Style Guide

RULE-ID: voice.business
Use concise professional prose. Preserve commitments, ownership, deadlines, quantities, and uncertainty.

RULE-ID: metrics.preserve
Keep currencies, percentages, fiscal periods, accounting signs, KPI names, and table totals exact.

RULE-ID: claims.scope
Do not strengthen forecasts, recommendations, risk statements, or legal/compliance language.

RULE-ID: terminology.first-use
Use approved organization, product, and role names consistently; retain the source name on first use when needed for auditability.
